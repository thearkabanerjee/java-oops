Write

a program that, given an array arr of n integers as input, prints the average of the positive integers in arr, followed by the average of the negative integers in arr. Sample input 1: 5 -10 12 12 -15 12 Output: 12.0 -12.5 Sample input 2: 4 -10 10 -10 10 Output: 10.0 -10.0
